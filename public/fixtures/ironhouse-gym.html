Membership terms

The Annual Commitment plan requires 12 consecutive monthly payments. Cancelling before the end of the commitment term incurs an early termination fee of $150. After the commitment term, your membership continues on a month-to-month basis and renews automatically.

Cancellation requests must be made in person at your home club or by certified mail.

An annual maintenance fee of $49 is charged on March 1 each year.
